# LavernAI Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the high-level vision and upcoming features for the LavernAI platform. 

> *Note: This roadmap is non-technical and focuses on product features and community enhancements. Implementation details are intentionally excluded.*

## Phase 1: Expanding the Foundation (Current)
* **Broader CBC Subject Coverage:** Expanding our question banks and past-paper repositories to cover all subjects within the Junior and Senior Secondary School Kenyan CBC curriculum.
* **Enhanced ELO & Arena System:** Introducing regional and school-based leaderboards to foster healthy, localized competition in the Arena.
* **Gem Economy Upgrades:** Expanding the utility of Gems (💎) so users have more ways to spend their earned Gems on premium platform features.

## Phase 2: Interactive Learning & SimCore
* **SimCore Growth:** Expanding our cutting-edge educational simulations (currently featuring Grade 12 Biology/Genetics) into Chemistry and Physics modules that align strictly with KICD syllabus requirements.
* **Accessibility Enhancements:** Deepening our commitment to inclusive design with WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance, accommodating neurodivergent and visually impaired students.
* **Offline Mode Enhancements:** Allowing users to download past papers and quizzes for offline practice to accommodate areas with limited internet connectivity.

## Phase 3: Community & Scholarships
* **Donation & Scholarship Portal Enhancements:** Upgrading the portal to allow sponsors to fund specific schools, regions, or even sponsor individual high-performing, low-income students directly through the platform.
* **Study Groups & Guilds:** Enabling students to form competitive teams and study groups to tackle collaborative challenges.
* **In-App Mentorship:** Connecting top-ranking university students with CBC students for virtual tutoring and mentorship.

*Disclaimer: This roadmap is subject to change based on user feedback, educational policy updates, and platform growth.*
