## What is LavernAI?

LavernAI is a premier knowledge-competition and learning platform built for Kenyan CBC (Competency-Based Curriculum) students, university scholars, and the general public. We blend education with gamification and an engaging virtual economy.

### Key Features
* **Subject-Based Competitions:** Test your knowledge across a wide array of subjects aligned with the Kenyan educational system.
* **The Arena & ELO Rankings:** Climb the leaderboards in competitive rooms and prove your intellectual mettle.
* **Gem Economy:** Earn Gems (💎) by winning matches, referring friends, and engaging with the platform. Use your Gems to access premium features and community posts! (Note: We use Gems for engagement and rewards; there are no live real-money payouts for winning matches).
* **SimCore Educational Simulations:** Interactive, cutting-edge simulations (such as our Grade 12 CBE Biology modules) that bring complex concepts to life.
* **Past-Papers Access:** A comprehensive library of past examination papers for robust revision.
* **Donation & Scholarship Portal:** A transparent avenue for sponsors and well-wishers to support students and fund scholarships.
